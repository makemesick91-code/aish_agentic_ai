# MVP Scope — Aish Agentic AI

Canonical: Master Source §47 (in scope), §48 (out of scope). Rule: `.claude/rules/02`. PRD §5, §10.

## In scope (Master Source §47)
Multi-tenant · multi-branch · user/role/permission · simple survey builder · CSAT · NPS · basic CES ·
QR survey · unique link · WhatsApp invitation link · email invitation · feedback inbox · AI sentiment ·
AI topic · AI severity · AI summary · recovery ticket · SLA · assignment · escalation · Google connection ·
Google location mapping · review sync · AI reply draft · human approval · publish reply · owner dashboard ·
branch dashboard · audit log · usage metering · basic subscription · platform admin · basic knowledge base ·
security foundation · backup & restore · basic observability.

## Out of scope for MVP (Master Source §48)
AI auto-refund/auto-compensation · fully autonomous complaint handling · auto-publish of all replies ·
voice agent · full AI call center · all social media at once · complex no-code workflow builder ·
integration marketplace · advanced churn prediction · full CDP · loyalty program · social publishing suite ·
advanced enterprise SSO · dedicated data warehouse · custom/white-label mobile app · advanced predictive
analytics · franchise royalty management · full marketing automation.

Anything out of scope requires a new versioned decision (`.claude/rules/12`) before it may be built.

## Guardrails
Foundation-first order (Master Source §62); manual workflow before automation; autonomy last
(`.claude/rules/05`). Non-goals for MVP are also enumerated in PRD §5.3.

**Status:** scope baseline documented. Implementation NOT STARTED.
