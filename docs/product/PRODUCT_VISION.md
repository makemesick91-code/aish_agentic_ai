# Product Vision — Aish Agentic AI

Canonical: Master Source §8–§14, §65. Rule: `.claude/rules/01`. Derived summary — see the Master Source for full text.

## What it is
**Aish Agentic AI** — a multi-tenant SaaS, Agentic AI **Customer Experience and Reputation Operating
Platform** managing CSAT / NPS / CES, feedback operations, customer recovery, Google Review management,
reputation, and multi-branch operational insight.

## Positioning
Not merely a survey app, CSAT tool, review dashboard, chatbot, or reply generator. It owns the full cycle:
customer interaction → transaction/service completed → feedback collection → AI analysis → risk/topic
classification → customer recovery → review management → operational insight → continuous improvement
(Master Source §9).

## Vision & mission
Become the operating system for customer experience, customer recovery, and online reputation for
multi-branch businesses — automatic, secure, measurable, and auditable (Master Source §10–§11). Help SMEs
use enterprise-grade technology while using AI safely and auditably.

## Problems solved
No central CSAT; feedback scattered across channels; complaints without a PIC or SLA; late/inconsistent
review replies; privacy-risky replies; no branch comparison or root-cause analysis; unrecovered unhappy
customers; no audit trail; ungoverned AI use (Master Source §12).

## Final product shape
CSAT/NPS/CES · Survey Builder · Campaign · Feedback Inbox · Customer Recovery · Google Business Profile
Integration · Google Review Management · AI Response Assistant · Reputation Analytics · Multi-Branch
Dashboard · Knowledge Base · Agentic Workflow · Notifications · Integrations · Subscription & Billing ·
Platform Admin Console · AI Operations Console · Security, Audit & Governance (Master Source §65).
