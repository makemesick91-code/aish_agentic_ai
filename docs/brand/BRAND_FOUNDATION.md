## 1. Non-claims (read first)

- **No final brand, logo, or design is claimed.** All content here is a working planning baseline.
- **Planning tokens are not implemented** in any running UI (see `VISUAL_IDENTITY_BASELINE.md`).
- **No misleading AI-autonomy claim.** Aish Agentic AI is agentic with humans in control; it is
  **not fully autonomous**, and marketing/branding **MUST NOT** imply full autonomy.
- **No guaranteed-rating claim.** Branding **MUST NOT** guarantee review-score, rating, or reputation
  outcomes. There is **no guaranteed** uplift in Google rating, CSAT, NPS, or CES.

## 2. Purpose and scope

The Brand Foundation fixes the permanent identity, positioning, promise, and truth boundaries for the
product **Aish Agentic AI**. Every downstream brand artifact — architecture, voice, tagline, visual
identity, accessibility, and logo governance — inherits the decisions recorded here. Sibling planning
documents are: `BRAND_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `BRAND_VOICE.md`, `WORKING_TAGLINE_DECISION.md`,
`VISUAL_IDENTITY_BASELINE.md`, `ACCESSIBILITY_BASELINE.md`, and `LOGO_AND_ASSET_GOVERNANCE.md`.

Scope covers naming, category, positioning, value pillars, audience framing, and the no-overclaim
boundary. It does **not** cover pricing, legal trademark filing, or final creative sign-off.

## 4. Category and positioning

Aish Agentic AI is positioned as an **Agentic AI Customer Experience and Reputation Operating
Platform**. Branding **MUST NOT** reduce the positioning to any of the narrow framings below.

| Reductive framing to avoid | Why it is wrong | Correct framing |
|----------------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| "A survey app" | Surveys are one input; recovery, AI, reputation, analytics are core | Customer Experience operating platform |
| "A review generator" | Implies review gating/manipulation, which is prohibited | Reputation management with equal, unmanipulated review access |
| "A chatbot" | Understates supervised multi-agent orchestration and human approval | Agentic AI with human-in-control governance |
| "A Google Review tool" | Google is one channel of many | Multi-channel CX + reputation platform |

---

## 5. Brand promise and value pillars

The brand promise: **help multi-branch businesses listen to every customer, recover unhappy ones,
and protect their reputation — with agentic AI that keeps humans in control.**

| # | Value pillar | What it means | Truth boundary |
|---|--------------|---------------|----------------|
| 1 | Customer Experience first | CSAT, NPS, CES, feedback, and recovery as one loop | Measures experience; does not fabricate satisfaction |
| 2 | Humans in control | Human approval on public/high-risk actions | AI drafts and suggests; people decide |
| 3 | Reputation integrity | Equal Google Review access for all eligible customers | No gating, no manipulation, no purchased reviews |
| 4 | Privacy by design | Personal/medical/financial data kept out of public output and AI where prohibited | Healthcare-safe by default |
| 5 | Evidence over hype | Truthful states, audit trails, measured metrics | Targets are hypotheses until measured |
| 6 | Works without AI | Manual fallback always available | Basic workflow does not depend on AI availability |

## 7. The no-overclaim boundary (mandatory)

Brand messaging is bound by the same truthfulness rules as the product. The following clauses are
non-negotiable and apply to every brand surface.

- Branding **MUST NOT** guarantee outcomes. There is **no guaranteed** improvement in rating, review
  volume, CSAT, NPS, CES, or revenue.
- Branding **MUST NOT** describe the product as **fully autonomous**. It is agentic with humans in
  control; the product is **not fully autonomous** and **MUST NOT** be marketed as replacing human
  judgment on public or high-risk actions.
- Branding **MUST NOT** claim capabilities that are not implemented and evidenced. Roadmap items are
  labeled as planned, never as shipped.
- Branding **MUST NOT** imply review gating, review manipulation, or preferential routing of only happy
  customers.
- Branding **MUST NOT** expose or imply access to personal, medical, financial, or sensitive
  transaction data in public output.

| Forbidden claim pattern | Reason | Compliant alternative |
|-------------------------|--------|-----------------------|
| "Guaranteed 5-star reputation" | Overclaim + gating implication | "Helps you respond to every review, with approval" |
| "Fully autonomous AI agents" | False autonomy claim | "Agentic AI with humans in control" |
| "AI replies published instantly" | Bypasses human approval | "AI drafts replies; your team approves before publishing" |
| "Boost your rating, guaranteed" | Guaranteed-rating overclaim | "Understand and act on feedback across branches" |

---

## 8. Brand truth statements

- Aish Agentic AI is a multi-tenant, multi-branch SaaS platform for Customer Experience and reputation.
- Aish Agentic AI uses supervised, multi-agent orchestration with mandatory human approval for
  public/high-risk actions.
- Aish Agentic AI treats customer feedback and reviews as untrusted input.
- Aish Agentic AI keeps the workflow usable when AI is unavailable.
- Aish Agentic AI records audit trails, AI tracing, and cost logging for accountability.
