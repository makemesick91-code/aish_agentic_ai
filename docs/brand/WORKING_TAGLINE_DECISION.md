## 1. Non-claims (read first)

- No final brand, logo, or design is claimed; this is a planning baseline.
- The recommended tagline is an **APPROVED WORKING BASELINE**, not a registered trademark and not a
  final creative commitment.
- Planning tokens are not implemented in any UI.
- No misleading AI-autonomy claim; no guaranteed-rating claim.

---

## 2. Purpose

Select a **working tagline** for Aish Agentic AI that is clear, differentiated, globally usable,
Indonesia-appropriate, healthcare-safe, multi-industry, and free of overclaim (no "fully autonomous",
no "guaranteed rating improvement"). The result is a working baseline for planning surfaces only.

---

## 3. Evaluation criteria

| Code | Criterion | Pass condition |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| C1 | Clarity | Understandable at a glance; no jargon |
| C2 | Differentiation | Distinguishes from survey/review/chatbot tools |
| C3 | Global usability | Works in English-speaking global markets |
| C4 | Indonesia usability | Clear and non-awkward for Indonesian audiences |
| C5 | Healthcare suitability | Safe for clinics; no medical overclaim |
| C6 | Multi-industry suitability | Not locked to one vertical |
| C7 | No overclaim | No guarantees; not "fully autonomous"; no guaranteed rating |

Scoring: ✓ pass, ~ partial, ✗ fail.

### 4.1 Candidate notes

- **#1** — Leads with the category (agentic customer experience) and the core safeguard (humans in
  control). Strong on differentiation and no-overclaim. Recommended.
- **#2** — Descriptive and safe, but reads more like a feature list than a positioning line.
- **#3** — Clear and safe but slightly generic; "AI that helps" is broadly used.
- **#4** — Strong recovery emphasis but narrows perception toward recovery only; "trusted" is slightly
  abstract for Indonesian audiences.
- **#5** — "Safely automated" risks implying more autonomy than the human-in-control model allows;
  partial on C5 and C7. Not recommended.

---

## 5. Recommended working tagline

> **Agentic customer experience, humans in control.**

**Decision status: APPROVED WORKING BASELINE.** This is the working tagline for planning and internal
alignment. It is **not** a trademark and **not** a final creative decision. Final selection requires
owner sign-off and (if pursued) trademark clearance.

### 5.1 Why it was selected

| Reason | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Category-accurate | Names "customer experience" and "agentic" without reducing to survey/review/chatbot |
| Truthful | "humans in control" directly encodes the human-approval governance; not fully autonomous |
| No overclaim | Makes no guarantee about ratings, reviews, or outcomes |
| Multi-industry | No vertical-specific terms; safe for healthcare and beyond |
| Bilingual-friendly | Short, concrete, and translatable for Indonesia → Global |

---

## 6. Usage rules

- Use the working tagline only on planning/internal surfaces until final approval; label it as a
  working baseline where appropriate.
- The tagline **MUST NOT** be paired with any guaranteed-outcome claim or any "fully autonomous" copy.
- The tagline **MUST NOT** replace the product name; Aish Agentic AI remains the product name.
- Localization **MUST** preserve the "humans in control" meaning; a translation that implies autonomy
  is non-compliant.

---

## 7. Rejected phrasings and why

To keep the working baseline defensible, the following phrasings were explicitly rejected and **MUST
NOT** be reintroduced as a tagline.

| Rejected phrasing | Reason for rejection |
|-------------------|----------------------|
| "Fully autonomous customer experience" | False autonomy claim; violates human-in-control governance |
| "Guaranteed 5-star reputation" | Guaranteed-rating overclaim and gating implication |
| "The AI that runs your reviews for you" | Implies unsupervised publishing without human approval |
| "Boost your rating with AI" | Implies guaranteed rating improvement |
| "Never lose a customer again" | Absolute promise that cannot be evidenced |
